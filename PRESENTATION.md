# Tembo Hotels — Power BI Capstone Presentation

**Suggested duration:** 3–4 minutes  
**Project:** Hotel revenue, guest experience, and operational performance analysis  
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI

## 1. Introduction (20–30 seconds)

Good morning, everyone.

My capstone project is **Tembo Hotels Revenue Analysis**, an interactive Power BI report designed to help hotel management understand three areas: **revenue performance, guest experience, and staff operations**.

The goal is to move beyond simply reporting numbers and identify opportunities for better business decisions. I'll take you through the three dashboard pages and close with my recommendations.

## 2. Revenue Analysis (50–60 seconds)

*Open Page 1: Revenue Analysis.*

The first page summarizes the hotel's financial performance.

- **Total revenue:** 7.70M
- **Average guest rating:** 2.99
- **Average nights stayed:** 2.97
- **Largest guest-city revenue source:** Nairobi at approximately 3.2M, followed by Eldoret at approximately 1.0M
- **Leading room categories by revenue:** Suite and Deluxe

The key finding is that **revenue is concentrated in certain customer markets and room categories**. Nairobi leads the city breakdown, while Suite and Deluxe rooms generate the highest revenue among room types.

For management, this suggests an opportunity to prioritize high-performing guest markets and review pricing and promotions for each room category. The page also allows us to filter results by date, payment method, location, and staff.

## 3. Guest Experience & Retention (50–60 seconds)

*Open Page 2: Guest Experience & Retention.*

The second page looks at who the guests are, where they come from, and how much they spend.

- **Total guests displayed:** 252
- **Average rating displayed:** 2.96
- **Average revenue per guest displayed:** 32.05K
- **Top 10 guests' combined spending:** 5.11M
- **Leading guest city:** Nairobi

The analysis shows that a relatively small group of **high-spending guests** accounts for a substantial amount of recorded spending. It also highlights geographic demand patterns through a map and city-level revenue breakdown.

The business opportunity is to improve service quality and explore targeted loyalty initiatives for high-value guests. **Actual retention or repeat-booking rates are not shown on this page**, so those would be useful metrics to add in a future iteration.

## 4. Operations & Staff Efficiency (50–60 seconds)

*Open Page 3: Operations & Staff Efficiency.*

The final page connects staff and department activity to bookings, revenue, and guest ratings.

- **Bookings displayed:** 252
- **Revenue displayed:** 7.70M
- **Average rating displayed:** 3.01
- **Highest department revenue attribution:** Front Desk, followed by Restaurant
- **Highest individual revenue attribution:** Brenda Achieng at approximately 1.28M
- **Highest individual average rating:** Joy Otieno at 3.47

This page gives managers a basis for identifying coaching needs, acknowledging strong performance, and examining how different departments contribute to results.

Importantly, revenue attribution should be considered alongside each employee's role, workload, and guest feedback—not used as a stand-alone performance ranking.

## 5. Recommendations and Conclusion (30–45 seconds)

Based on these findings, I recommend three actions:

1. **Focus revenue efforts** on high-performing markets such as Nairobi while testing room-category pricing and promotions.
2. **Improve the guest experience** by reviewing customer feedback and exploring loyalty initiatives for high-value guests.
3. **Support operational performance** through staff coaching, recognition, and ongoing monitoring of bookings, ratings, and revenue.

In conclusion, this project demonstrates how Power BI can turn hotel data into an interactive management tool that helps answer **what is happening, where the opportunities are, and what action to take next**.

Thank you.

## Presenter Notes — Validate Before Presenting

These are **report checks**, not additional findings:

- The report displays different average-rating values across the pages (**2.99**, **2.96**, and **3.01**). Confirm whether this is due to different measures or filter contexts.
- The **32.05K** average revenue-per-guest figure does not directly equal **7.70M ÷ 252**. Confirm the measure definition and underlying denominator.
- The revenue-by-month chart is visually ordered by revenue rather than by date; avoid presenting the displayed left-to-right order as a chronological trend.
- Some slicers labeled **Room Type** show city values; verify labels and fields before the final demo.
- The report does not explicitly label the revenue **currency**, so avoid naming one until confirmed.
